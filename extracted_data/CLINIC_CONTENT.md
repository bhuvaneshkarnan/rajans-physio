## 4. Services & Treatment Modalities

1. **Orthopedic & Sports Manual Therapy**
   - Hands-on joint manipulation, mobilization, chiropractic alignment, and soft tissue mobilization to restore mechanics and range of motion.
2. **Dorn Method Spinal Alignment**
   - A gentle, non-invasive vertebrae alignment technique without cracking or surgery, targeting spinal subluxations, disc compression, sciatica, neck ache, and scoliosis.
3. **Advanced Laser & Scanning Laser Therapy**
   - High-intensity therapeutic laser that penetrates deep soft tissues to reduce acute/chronic inflammation, stimulate ATP cellular repair, and relieve deep tendon and ligament pain.
4. **Radial Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy (ESWT)**
   - Non-invasive acoustic wave therapy specifically effective for chronic calcifications, heel spurs, plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, and stubborn tendinopathies.
5. **TECAR Therapy (Radiofrequency Diathermy)**
   - Radiofrequency-based treatment used as an adjunct to musculoskeletal rehabilitation, increasing microcirculation and relaxing severe muscle spasms.
6. **Clinical Dry Needling (CDNT - USA)**
   - Precision filament needle insertion into taut neuromuscular trigger points to instantly eliminate deep muscular knots, spasm, and referred nerve pain.
7. **Kinesio Cupping Therapy & IASTM**
   - Dynamic negative-pressure cupping and Instrument-Assisted Soft Tissue Mobilization to break up scar tissue, release fascial restrictions, and enhance lymph drainage.
8. **Acupuncture & Acupressure**
   - Holistic meridian and electro-acupuncture for neuromuscular balance, neuropathic pain management, and systemic relief.
9. **Electrotherapy Modalities**
   - Interferential Therapy (IFT), Ultrasonic Therapy (UST), Electrical Muscle Stimulation (EMS), Shortwave Diathermy (SWD), and Cryotherapy.
10. **Kinesio Taping (CKT)**
    - Functional neuromuscular taping to support weak joints, decrease swelling, improve proprioception, and protect muscles during rehabilitation.
11. **Varma Therapy**
    - Traditional Indian vital point therapy practiced for neurological, neuromuscular, and chronic pain alleviation.
12. **Home Physiotherapy Visits**
    - Comprehensive in-home care for patients unable to visit the clinic.
