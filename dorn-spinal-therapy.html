Why the Dorn Method Excels over Conventional Manipulation

In conventional manipulation, the joint is thrust while the surrounding musculature is passive, triggering a reflexive defensive spasm. In the Dorn Method, Dr. Prabhu Rajan guides the patient into controlled rhythmic movements (such as swinging a leg or arm). This distracts the muscular neuromuscular guard, allowing the vertebrae to slip effortlessly back into place without trauma or ligament strain.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dorn spinal therapy painful or does it involve violent cracking? +
No. Dorn therapy involves zero sudden, high-velocity cracking or twisting. It is a gentle, bio-mechanical mobilization technique performed while you move your limb. Patients frequently report immediate sensation of lightness and relief.
How many Dorn sessions will I typically need? +
Most acute spinal and pelvic alignment issues experience significant improvement within 2 to 4 sessions. Chronic long-standing disc conditions may require 5 to 8 sessions combined with self-help home exercises.
Can older adults with osteoporosis receive Dorn therapy? +
Yes. Because there are no forceful thrusts, Dorn is one of the safest modalities for elderly patients, osteopenic individuals, and sensitive spines.
