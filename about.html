Founder & Chief Physiotherapist

Healing through root-cause science, empathy & dedication since 2009.

Founded by Dr. Prabhu Rajan, PT in Chromepet, Chennai, Rajan's Physiotherapy & Pain Relief Centre was built on a foundational promise: treating the whole human being, not just a pain symptom on a prescription pad.

Over 17 years of continuous clinical practice, Dr. Prabhu Rajan has spearheaded advanced conservative orthopedic rehabilitation in Chennai—serving as Vice Chairman of the All India Dorn Therapy Association (AIDTA) and pioneering non-surgical spinal realignments.
